Who we are

About J.K Hospital

J.K Hospital is a hospital in Tanuku, registered in 2015 (Regd. No. 493/18/2015), caring for patients with chest and lung conditions, patients who need general or laparoscopic surgery, and anyone who needs emergency care.

A duty doctor is on site 24 hours a day, supported by an ICU, ventilator, central oxygen and a 24-hour laboratory, so urgent cases can be seen at any hour.

  • Chest & lung careAsthma, breathing and lung conditions, with PFT testing.
  • General & laparoscopic surgeryModern operation theatre with HD laparoscopy.
  • Emergency & critical care24-hour duty doctor, ICU and ventilator.
  • Insurance supportCashless and reimbursement facilities.
